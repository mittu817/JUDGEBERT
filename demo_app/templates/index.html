📄 JUDGEBERT: Assessing Legal Meaning Preservation Between Sentences (EMNLP 2025)

JUDGEBERT is the first model and benchmark dataset (FrJUDGE) dedicated to the rigorous assessment of legal meaning preservation during text simplification of insurance policies and statutory contracts.

Unlike general text simplification tasks where readability is the primary objective, in legal domains, omitting a condition, broadening an exclusion, or altering compensation timelines invalidates the legal integrity of the document and creates liability risks.

Key Contributions:

  • FrJUDGE Dataset: 1,485 certified annotations by 5 legal experts across 247 real-world contracts.
  • Multi-Vector ColBERT MaxSim: Token-to-token late interaction semantic alignment capturing subtle nuance.
  • Neuro-Symbolic Guardrails: Deterministic verification of party roles, causality direction, deontic modalities, and quantitative terms.
  • Closed-Loop Auto-Repair: Surgical clause reconstruction with certified meaning preservation without verbatim copying.
